IGNIS UY — HISTORICAL PILOT 2000–2026

ignis_historico.py is an INDEPENDENT Streamlit application.
It does not replace or modify the original IGNIS UY 1.7 version.

To test locally:
  pip install -r requirements.txt
  streamlit run ignis_historico.py

For Streamlit Cloud, create a NEW app/repository with ignis_historico.py as its entry point.
Do NOT overwrite streamlit_app.py in the original repository.

Live NASA FIRMS data:
- The upload tab can retrieve recent Uruguay NRT detections without uploading ZIP/CSV files.
- Obtain a free NASA FIRMS MAP_KEY and configure FIRMS_MAP_KEY in Streamlit Cloud Secrets.
- Locally, copy .streamlit/secrets.toml.example to .streamlit/secrets.toml and replace the example value.
- Queries span up to the previous 10 days and are cached for one hour.

Current limitations:
- Reads historical ZIP/CSV files and queries NASA FIRMS NRT data.
- Supports MODIS observations from 2000 without VIIRS.
- Does not automatically download 27 years of records.
- Uploads are subject to Streamlit Cloud file-size limits.
- A month without imported observations proves neither an absence of detections nor full satellite coverage.
- The map shows thermal-point density, not confirmed wildfires.
- Reports and evidence for 2023 remain in the original app.
- Raw sums across sensors are not a scientifically harmonized historical series.
